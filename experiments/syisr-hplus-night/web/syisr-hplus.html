SYISR nighttime zenith: H⁺ mixture fit

2023-11-15, 16:00–17:00 UTC · Sanya monostatic zenith · uncoded 480 µs long pulse · independent Rust processing.

Proof of concept: the thermal H⁺/He⁺/O⁺ model favors an H⁺ number fraction of 0.24 near 700 km, averaged over a roughly 100 km pulse/range footprint. The 200 whole-dwell resamples span 0.227–0.267 between the 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. This interval is conditional on the thermal mixture and inferred receiver response; it is not the total systematic uncertainty.

Processing and interpretation

  • 19,849 of 20,625 zenith IPPs retained. A conservative per-IPP range-power screen rejected 776 candidate contaminated pulses before averaging; this screen is not an identified-satellite catalogue or range–Doppler matched-filter classifier.
  • H⁺ and He⁺ fractions are fitted, with the remainder O⁺. No supplied L2 fit or IRI composition is used as fitting input.
  • Fit measured complex lag products through the finite pulse, inferred voltage-filter response and local exponential power gradient. This is a local footprint model, not a full-profile composition inversion.
  • Exact magnetized Maxwellian electron response; large-kρ ion limit tested for this zenith geometry, 25.2° from perpendicular at 700 km. Collisionless topside approximation; no collision frequency is fitted. A Rust MSIS neutral-collision scale check is included.
  • Absolute electron-density calibration is unavailable. Native power is a nuisance amplitude; Debye-density sensitivity refits are retained. The physical Doppler sign is not independently calibrated.
  • Whole-dwell resampling preserves the measured lag/range vectors. Fits to individual dwells also allow temperature and He⁺ variation. Full range/lag covariance and receiver uncertainty are not comprehensively marginalized.

Data and source

Named float32 HDF5 datasets (about 74 kB) · Fits, likelihood profiles and ACF diagnostics · Whole-dwell resampling and joint-profile checks · Synthetic recovery tests · MSIS check

The HDF5 file contains h_fraction, h_fraction_conditionally_supported, quality_flag, temperatures, measured/model ACFs, units, UTC bounds and provenance. Unsupported fractions are NaN only in the explicitly filtered dataset; all fitted values and signed measurements remain available.

Scientific status: first nighttime zenith proof of concept. Unscreened six-hour exploratory fits are retained on the compute archive, not promoted as validated composition products.
